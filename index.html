    Where the numbers come from

    outbid.lol publishes each top listing's lifetime paid total and lifetime click count. We read the public page every 5 minutes — one request, nothing behind a login — and divide one by the other.

    What $/click means here

    Lifetime dollars over lifetime clicks. It is not the "clicks" counter on outbid.lol's rows (that one resets with bids). Lower is a better deal. It says nothing about what those clicks were worth to the buyer.

    What's missing

    outbid.lol only publishes click counts for its top spenders — the long tail of the board can't be deal-ranked. If a figure looks wrong, it gets re-read and corrected; we don't take money to change a number.
