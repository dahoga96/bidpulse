Where the numbers come from

Every board publishes its leaderboard as public HTML — bid amounts and, on most of them, a click counter per listing. We read the page. Nothing private, nothing scraped behind a login.

How $ / click is calculated

The #1 listing's total bid divided by the clicks that listing reports. It is the cheapest honest comparison across boards, and it is usually much worse than the operator implies.

What flatlined means

No new bid in 12 hours. A board with no bidding has no audience, which means a slot on it delivers nothing regardless of what it costs.

Corrections

Operators can dispute any figure and we will re-read the page and publish the correction. We do not take money to change a number.
